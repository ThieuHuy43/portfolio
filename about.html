About Me

Hi! I'm Huy, an AI Engineer focused on AI agents, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), and LLM systems. I love turning research ideas into working, deployed products — from data pipelines to agent orchestration to production APIs.

Background

I'm a final-year AI & Data Science student at Thuy Loi University (GPA 3.33/4.0), and over the past two years I've moved from computer vision research into building production AI agent and RAG systems. My work spans model training and evaluation, retrieval pipeline design, and shipping agentic applications with real backend integrations.

My graduation project, VietTraffic, is an Agentic RAG platform for Vietnamese traffic law consulting — built with LangGraph orchestrating Gemini, DeepSeek, and Groq, processing 3,000+ semantic legal chunks with Qdrant hybrid search and a human-in-the-loop dashboard to keep every answer verified.

What I Work On

AI Agents & MCP Tools

At Meta Neurons, I build and manage MCP tools and design agent workflows using the Claude SDK and Agno, integrating backend APIs, caching, and prompt engineering to improve agent reliability — plus benchmark evaluations to compare agent performance.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

I've built RAG systems end-to-end: an agricultural consulting chatbot at IEIA (ChromaDB, 92% Top-5 retrieval recall) and VietTraffic's legal QA pipeline (Qdrant, hierarchical chunking, reranking). I care about grounding — reducing hallucination through better chunking, retrieval, and prompt design.

Computer Vision & Research

My research background is in object detection and vision-language models (YOLOv8/v11, CLIP, ViT). My paper AMAF-Net, on training-free multi-modal alignment for fine-grained counterfeit fruit detection, won the Best Paper Award at ICAI 2025.

What Drives Me

I value end-to-end thinking — from data collection and retrieval design to deployment and monitoring. I believe great AI systems need more than a good model: they need thoughtful engineering, rigorous evaluation, and reliable production infrastructure.
